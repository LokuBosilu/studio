# **App Name**: Laya Art Gallery

## Core Features:

- Interactive Art Gallery: Display a wide range of artworks with zoom and detailed views.
- E-commerce Integration: Enable direct purchasing options for artworks via a simple checkout process.
- Artist Application Portal: Allow artists to apply to sell their work through a dedicated application form.
- About Us Section: Provide background information about the gallery, its mission, and featured artists.
- AI-Powered Artwork Description: Leverage AI to generate engaging and informative descriptions for each artwork using a tool.
- Virtual Exhibition Preview: Provide users with AI-generated previews of how artwork will look in various virtual environments before purchase using a tool.

## Style Guidelines:

- Background color: Soft beige (#F5F5DC) to provide a warm and inviting atmosphere.
- Primary color: Muted gold (#C2B280) to add a touch of elegance and sophistication.
- Accent color: Burnt umber (#8A3324) for highlights and call-to-action buttons, complementing the earthy tones.
- Body text: 'Literata', serif. For readability and a classic, literary feel.
- Headlines: 'Belleza', sans-serif. Pairs well with 'Alegreya' and gives a stylish feel. 
- Use minimalist line icons in gold to represent different art categories and functionalities.
- Employ a grid-based layout to showcase artworks in a visually appealing and organized manner.
